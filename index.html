Design engineering

Product Highlights

An embeddable widget that surfaces key product facts — delivery, guarantee, social proof, material, fit — across a merchant's product page.

One script, one payload, four placements and six layouts. No dependencies, no build step, everything inside shadow roots.

Demos

  • Product page The mock storefront with the widget mounted. A theme panel on the right edge configures the tokens and switches layout live.
  • Dev harness Every layout, plus the degradation states — ragged content, an empty array, a malformed payload.
  • Stress suite Seven host pages built to break it: hostile CSS, transformed ancestors, a dark theme, a strict CSP, broken payloads.
  • React variant The same widget rebuilt on React 19 and Motion 12, so the stack decision rests on a measurement rather than an assertion.

Reasoning, trade-offs and the architecture decision records are in the repository.
