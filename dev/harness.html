Product Highlights — dev harness

Every layout, every content shape and every way the payload can be wrong, on one page. Each case states what should be true, so a wrong result is obvious rather than merely different.

Layouts6

One payload, one layout option. The five single-surface layouts are the same fallback rule an explicit toast: false travels, so nothing is dropped — every item lands in the mount.

01

Distributed default

Four placements: three items beside the buy action, the badge on its anchor, the social proof in a floating toast. The only case here that renders the toast — the rest suppress it so it fires from one place.

Rows stagger in, then a warm sweep crosses the toast's first line once, left to right. The toast leads with the avatar stack and rotates between its title and its body; the same item shows a star in every other case, which is toastIcon doing its one job. No badge here — this page has no anchor for it, so it demotes to a list row.

02

Inline list

Every item in one list, titles and bodies. layout: 'list'.

Five rows. The toast and badge items return to the list rather than floating, and no surface was suppressed by hand to make that happen.

03

Compact grid

Two columns, mark above title above body. layout: 'compact'.

Five cells across two columns, each with its body. Nothing floating.

04

Super simple

Three columns, mark and title only. layout: 'simple'.

Five cells across three columns, no bodies at all. Titles should not break at a hyphen.

05

Accordion

Titles upfront, bodies behind a disclosure, one open at a time. layout: 'accordion'. Deliberately ragged: the last item has no body.

Six rows separated by hairlines. Clicking one opens it and closes any other. Tab reaches each header; Enter and Space both work. The last row has nothing to reveal, so it is not a button and has no chevron.

06

Animated steps

The accordion, cycling its rows on its own. layout: 'steps'.

Rows open in turn and wrap round after the last, each with a fill on its bottom edge sized to its dwell. Hovering holds the current row and resumes on what is left of it, not a fresh one; a hidden tab pauses it entirely. Clicking ends the sequence for good and leaves a plain accordion. Under reduced motion it never plays — row one is simply open.

09

Ragged content

A missing body, an icon name we do not ship, and copy far longer than any merchant should send.

Row one is title-only. Row two falls back to a neutral glyph rather than a gap. Row three wraps rather than clipping. None of it breaks layout.

10

Rating panel

Score drawn from rating and scale, never parsed out of the sentence beside it.

4.8 of 5 fills four and four-fifths stars — not rounded up to five. The second entry has no usable score, so it demotes to a list row rather than rendering an empty panel.

Failure modes2

A third-party widget has no business taking a merchant's page down with it. Both of these should leave the page exactly as they found it.

11

Empty array

Nothing renderable at all.

The frame below is flat and empty — no box, no stray rule, no reserved space.

12

Malformed payload

Items that are strings, nulls, numbers, and objects with no title.

Only the one valid item renders. Everything else is dropped rather than repaired. One console warning at most, and no exception.
