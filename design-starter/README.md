# Starter Materials

Two files to work with:

- **host-page.html**: a mock merchant product page. It has its own styling, including some generically named classes (`.card`, `.title`, `.btn`, `.container`) that are a realistic hazard. Your widget should live inside a page like this, feel at home, and neither break the page nor be broken by it. There is a `#widget-slot` you may mount into, though you can place your widget wherever it works best.

- **sample-content.json**: the content your widget should render. Treat it as data supplied per merchant; your widget should be driven by it, not hard-coded to it.

You are free to restructure or extend these as needed for your submission, but your widget should demonstrably work when dropped into a page like the one provided.
